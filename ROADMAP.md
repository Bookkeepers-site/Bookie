# The Bookkeepers Solution — Phase One Roadmap

**Goal:** Build The Bookkeepers Solution into Australia's most innovative bookkeeping business. Not the biggest. The smartest.

**First milestone:** A$100,000 — then keep going.

---

## The Vision

When someone lands on the website, they don't see another bookkeeping firm. They think: *"These people actually solve problems."*

They can:
- Get their books rescued
- Improve cashflow
- Learn to use AI
- Buy practical tools
- Access helpful resources
- Eventually subscribe to software
